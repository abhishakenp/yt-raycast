# Design system reference: institutional / public sector (base)

## Role

Accessibility, calm hierarchy, service paths, notices, and transparent contact — not startup hype.

## Minimum sections (thin prompts)

Header with crest/wordmark placeholder + utility links → hero with mission/service line → 2–4 service tiles → news or notices strip → contact/access row → footer with required link groups.

## Anti-patterns

Fake government seals or registration IDs; aggressive growth marketing tone.

## Tokens

High contrast options; large click targets; restrained color.

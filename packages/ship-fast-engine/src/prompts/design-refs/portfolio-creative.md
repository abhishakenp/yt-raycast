# Design system reference: portfolio creative director / photo

## Role

Large visuals, project titles with year/client type, restrained copy, contact that invites inquiry.

## Minimum sections (thin prompts)

Full-bleed hero or split → masonry or consistent-ratio grid (≥8) → about with philosophy → contact + social row → footer minimal.

## Anti-patterns

Generic SaaS features; stock laptop mockups dominating non-tech creative work without prompt.

## Tokens

Editorial serif optional; ample negative space; image-first rhythm.

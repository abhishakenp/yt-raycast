Frequently Asked Questions

How does Chat Collector store my data?

All chats are saved locally on your machine in an encrypted SQLite database. No data is sent to external servers unless you explicitly export it.

Can I import chats from other platforms?

Yes! The Import page supports OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, and can be extended via plugins.

Is there a limit to how many chats I can archive?

The only limit is the storage capacity of your device. The app is optimized to handle tens of thousands of conversations efficiently.

How do I delete a conversation permanently?

Open the conversation in the Dashboard, click the three‑dot menu, and select “Delete”. This removes it from local storage and, if the provider supports it, issues a remote delete request.

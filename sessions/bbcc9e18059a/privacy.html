Privacy Policy

Your Data, Your Control

Chat Collector is built on the principle that your AI conversations belong to you. We never upload, store, or share your chats on external servers. All data is kept locally on the device you run the application on, unless you explicitly choose to export it.

By using this service you agree that you are the sole owner of the content you import. We provide tools to help you manage, delete, and back up your data, but we do not retain any copies beyond what you store.

What Information We Collect

  • Chat Content: Full message history, timestamps, and any attachments you import from supported providers.
  • Provider Tokens: API keys or OAuth tokens are stored only in your browser’s local storage and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Usage Metrics (Optional): If you enable anonymous usage analytics, we collect aggregated counts of feature usage. This can be turned off in Settings.

How We Protect Your Data

All data is encrypted at rest using the Web Crypto API when you enable the optional encryption feature. The encryption key never leaves your device. Communication with third‑party providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini) occurs over HTTPS, and we only request the minimal scopes needed to fetch your conversation history.

We regularly audit the client‑side code for security vulnerabilities and encourage you to keep your browser up to date.

Your Rights

  • Delete any or all of your stored chats at any time via the Dashboard.
  • Export your entire archive in JSON format for personal backup.
  • Revoke provider access tokens from the Settings page or directly from the provider’s portal.
  • Opt‑out of anonymous usage analytics in Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Version” number at the bottom. Continued use of the application after changes indicates acceptance of the new terms.

Your data stays local. No cloud. No tracking. Always private.

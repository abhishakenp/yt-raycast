About Chat Collector

Your private hub for AI conversation history.

Our Mission

In a world where AI assistants become daily collaborators, we believe your conversation data should stay under your control. Chat Collector was built to give you a secure, searchable archive of every chat you have with OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, and any future provider.

By keeping everything local, we eliminate the risk of unwanted data collection while still providing powerful tools to tag, search, and export your threads.

Key Features

  • Automatic import from major AI providers.
  • Full‑text search with instant results.
  • Tagging and categorisation for easy organisation.
  • Local‑only storage – no cloud, no third‑party access.
  • Export to JSON, Markdown or plain text.

Add a New Provider

Want to see another AI service in Chat Collector? Follow the steps below:

  1. Open the Settings page.
  2. Click “Add Provider” and select the service.
  3. Enter your API key or OAuth credentials.
  4. Save – the provider will appear here once the connection is verified.

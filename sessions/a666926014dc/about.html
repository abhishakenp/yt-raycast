Our Story

Lidmi started as a simple idea: bring people together through real‑world experiences. Today we’re a global community of explorers, creators, and friends who believe that the best moments happen offline.

Our Mission

To make it effortless for anyone to create, discover, and join events that spark genuine connections. We empower hosts with tools that are simple yet powerful, and we give participants a safe space to explore new interests.

Our Vision

A world where every city, town, and neighborhood has a thriving calendar of community‑driven experiences. Where technology serves as the bridge, not the barrier, to real‑life interaction.

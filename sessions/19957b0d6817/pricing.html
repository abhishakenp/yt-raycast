Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex) and PayPal. Enterprise customers can arrange invoicing.

Can I cancel or downgrade my plan?

Yes. You can downgrade or cancel at any time from your account settings. Refunds are issued on a prorated basis.

We provide a 7‑day free trial with 100 generations so you can explore the full feature set before committing.

All generated images and prompts are stored securely and are not used to train our models unless you opt‑in.

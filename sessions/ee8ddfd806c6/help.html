Getting Started

New to Chat Collector? Follow these quick steps to import your first conversation.

  1. Connect a provider from the Settings page.
  2. Click “Start Importing” on the Home screen.
  3. Choose the date range and tags you want to sync.
  4. Review the imported chats in the Dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my data stored?

All chats are saved locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your device. No data is sent to external servers unless you explicitly export it.

Can I sync across multiple devices?

Yes. Export your archive from one device and import it on another using the “Export Archive” and “Import Archive” options in the Settings page.

What providers are supported?

Currently we support OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), and Google Gemini. More integrations are planned for future releases.

How do I delete a conversation?

Open the conversation in the Dashboard, click the trash icon next to the title, and confirm the deletion.

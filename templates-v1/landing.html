Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out to us.

How fast are deployments, really?
Our median deploy time is 2.8 seconds from git push to live. This includes building, optimizing assets, and propagating to all 300+ edge locations. We achieve this through incremental builds and a custom-built deployment pipeline that parallelizes every step.
Can I migrate from my current hosting provider?
Absolutely. We provide a one-command migration CLI that imports your projects, environment variables, domains, and DNS records. Most teams complete the migration in under 15 minutes. We also support custom Docker containers if you have non-standard setups.
What frameworks and languages do you support?
We have first-class support for Next.js, Remix, Astro, SvelteKit, Nuxt, and any Node.js framework. We also support Go, Rust, Python, and Ruby through custom build packs. If it runs in a container, it runs on Velocity.

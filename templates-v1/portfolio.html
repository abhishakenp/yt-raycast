About

Designing with
intention & care

I'm a product designer and creative director based in Barcelona with over 10 years of experience shaping digital products and brand identities for startups and established companies alike.

My approach is rooted in empathy and clarity. I believe that great design doesn't just look beautiful -- it solves real problems, tells compelling stories, and creates moments of delight that people remember.

Previously, I've led design at Figment Studio, Arclight Digital, and Cohere Labs, working with clients ranging from early-stage startups to Fortune 500 brands.
